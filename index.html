Ask on WhatsApp
Do you only arrange trips in Dubai?

No. We're based in Dubai and plan holidays to destinations around the world, from Georgia and Turkey to the Maldives, Europe and Southeast Asia. We also run tours across Dubai and the UAE.

Can you help with visas for other countries?

Yes. We help with visa applications for many popular destinations, as well as UAE tourist visas. Share your nationality and travel dates and we'll tell you which documents you need.

Can I book just a flight or a hotel?

Yes. You can book flights, hotels, travel insurance or transfers on their own, or let us put everything together as one package.

Can you build a custom package?

Yes. Tell us where you'd like to go, your dates, your budget and who is travelling, and we'll put together an itinerary that fits.

Do your Dubai tours include hotel pickup?

Most of our Dubai tours include pickup and drop-off from hotels and residences in Dubai. We confirm your pickup time when you book.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp or give us a call. We'll reply with options and confirm every detail in writing before you pay.
